# NovaTutor GATE CSE Module

Added as a frontend-only study module on top of Checkpoint 11.

## Included
- Navbar entry: GATE CSE
- GATE 2026 syllabus mapped to 10 CS sections + General Aptitude
- Subject-wise original exam-focused PDF notes
- Complete syllabus PDF
- Official GATE previous-paper/master-paper links
- 50 original PYQ-pattern practice questions with answers/explanations
- Downloadable 50-question test-sheet PDF
- NPTEL supplementary learning links
- Responsive UI, tabs, cards, animations and interactive test flow

## Source policy
The in-app notes and practice questions are original NovaTutor material aligned to the official syllabus. Official previous-year papers are linked to the GATE organizing institutes rather than copied into the app. NPTEL links are supplementary learning references.

Official references:
- https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/exam-papers-and-syllabus.html
- https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/download.html
- https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/QPs-answer-keys.html
- https://gate2025.iitr.ac.in/question-papers.html
